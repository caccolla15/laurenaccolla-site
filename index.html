Mary’s Blue Blanket

A Gentle Story for Children About Love, Miscarriage, and God’s Eternal Care

How do you explain the loss of a baby to a young child?

Mary’s Blue Blanket is a tender Catholic picture book written to help families navigate the heartbreak of miscarriage together.

Told through the eyes of a young child, the story explores the questions and emotions that can follow the loss of a baby. Through gentle, age-appropriate language and the comforting image of Mary’s blue mantle, children are reminded that their feelings matter, their baby will always be part of their family, and they are surrounded by love even in sadness.

Written with young children and their families in mind, Mary’s Blue Blanket offers a gentle way to begin difficult conversations, honor the life of a beloved baby, and find hope and comfort through faith.

Meet Lauren

I’m a Catholic wife, mother of three (and four in Heaven), licensed marriage and family therapist, and children’s book author.

For more than a decade, I’ve had the privilege of walking alongside individuals and families as they navigate grief, anxiety, relationships, and life’s difficult experiences. My own family’s journey through miscarriage is the heart behind Mary’s Blue Blanket.
