About Lauren

The person and the inspiration behind Mary’s Blue Blanket.

The Heart Behind Mary’s Blue Blanket

My husband and I have experienced the heartbreak of multiple miscarriages, and along that journey, we found ourselves facing questions that many grieving parents encounter: How do we talk to our children about a baby they will never meet? How do we help them understand death while still protecting their innocence? How do we make space for their grief while navigating our own?

As both a mother and a therapist, I knew how important it was to speak honestly and concretely with children in ways they could understand. Yet I also longed for a resource that would gently weave together the realities of miscarriage, the love of family, and the comfort of our Catholic faith.

That longing eventually became Mary’s Blue Blanket.

I wrote this story for the children who have questions, the parents searching for the right words, and the families learning how to carry both grief and hope. My prayer is that this book helps families feel seen, opens the door to meaningful conversations, and offers a gentle reminder that even in sorrow, we are never alone.

Meet Lauren

I’m a Catholic wife, mother of three (and four in Heaven), licensed marriage and family therapist, and children’s book author.

For more than a decade, I’ve had the privilege of walking alongside individuals and families as they navigate grief, anxiety, relationships, and life’s difficult experiences. Through my work as a therapist, I’ve seen how important it is for children to have the words, reassurance, and support they need to make sense of challenging situations.

As a licensed marriage and family therapist in Colorado, I own and operate Your Faithful Therapist, a private practice where I integrate evidence-based therapeutic approaches with the Catholic faith for those who desire it. My faith shapes how I understand love, suffering, and hope, and I believe it can offer a beautiful source of comfort during life’s most difficult moments.
